Framework Layer Status

Layer 1: Geospatial Ingestion Engine
What it means: Combines Satellite & Ground Data. Merges satellite gravity (GRACE), rainfall, soil moisture, and ground wells into a 4km x 4km grid.
Tech: GRACE TWSA • GLDAS Fluxes • Cubic Spline Interpolation • Z-Score Norm
Layer 2: Multi-Scale Downscaling Engine
What it means: Zooms In on Satellite Images. Converts coarse 1° (~110km) satellite data down to sharp 4km resolution without losing accuracy.
Tech: Random Forest & XGBoost Ensemble • Scale Preservation R_scale • GWLA Converter
Layer 3: Unsaturated Vadose Zone Delay Model
What it means: Models Rain Infiltration Lag. Simulates rain soaking through soil layer before reaching groundwater aquifer table.
Tech: Gamma Delay Kernel Convolution • Effective Recharge R_eff
Layer 4: Scientific ML Dynamic Surrogates
What it means: Fast Physics AI Simulator. AI models (GNN-LSTM, PINN, FNO) predict water table changes 100x faster than physics equations.
Tech: Spatio-Temporal GNN-LSTM • 2D Dupuit-Boussinesq PINN • 2D Fourier Neural Operator
Layer 5: Finite-Sample Conformal Predictor
What it means: Guaranteed Safety Margin. Calculates 95% confidence bounds (q̂ = 0.496m) to prevent dry wells during droughts.
Tech: Split Conformal UQ • Non-Conformity Scores • Empirical Quantile q_hat
Layer 6: SCP Chance-Constrained MPC Optimizer
What it means: Smart Pumping Quota Optimizer. Recommends safe monthly pumping limits for agricultural & municipal wells.
Tech: Two-Loop SCP Solver • ECOS Optimizer • Chance-Constrained MPC

System Help & User Guide

1. How to Predict Groundwater Levels

1. Select a Hydrogeological Region Domain (e.g. Illinois, North German Basin, High Plains, Indo-Gangetic Basin).

2. Enter your query Latitude and Longitude coordinates into the formula bar.

3. Choose your Data Source (Synthetic Benchmark Engine or Realtime NASA EarthData / GEE API).

4. Click FETCH to run predictions, update spatial heatmaps, 3D WebGL models, and conformal bounds.

2. Navigating Maps & 3D WebGL Models

• Interactive GIS Map: Toggle between Terrain, Hydro, & Satellite layers. Click anywhere on the map to pin query coordinates.

• 3D WebGL Aquifer Visualizer: Click and drag to rotate 360°, scroll to zoom. Visualizes Topography, Water Table, Sea Level (0.0m MSL), and Bedrock Floor.

• Spatial Drawdown Raster: 16x16 grid heatmap displaying hydraulic head distribution.

3. Safety Limits & Pumping Quota Optimization

• Split Conformal UQ: Calculates a distribution-free safety buffer (q̂ = 0.496m) to prevent well depletion during droughts.

• SCP CCMPC Optimizer: Click Optimize Pumping Quotas under Parameters to solve sustainable monthly extraction limits ($E^*$).

4. Generating Location Reports

• Once you click FETCH, the Generate Report button appears in the top formula bar.

• Click Generate Report to view and print a comprehensive geodetic location assessment report.

Webpage User Guide & Workflow Instructions

How to navigate, configure forecasts, and optimize pumping quotas

1

Top Formula Bar (Data Ingestion & Location)

Select a hydrogeological region (e.g. Ganges Basin, Ogallala Aquifer) or enter custom (Latitude, Longitude) coordinates. Choose between Synthetic Benchmark data or Realtime NASA/GEE API, then click FETCH to run localized predictions. Click Generate Report for a dynamic printable PDF report.

2

Panel 1: Parameters & Framework Layer Status

Expand Panel 1 to configure SCP CCMPC Optimizer parameters (Horizon N, h_min, h_target, α). Inspect the 7-Layer Scientific Machine Learning pipeline status (Geospatial Ingestion, GRACE Downscaling, Vadose Delay, GNN-LSTM Surrogates, Split Conformal Calibration, SCP Solver, and Feature Ablation Benchmarks).

3

Panel 2: Interactive Map & 3D WebGL Aquifer Model

Expand Panel 2 to view Leaflet GIS spatial drawdown heatmaps, piezometric monitoring wells, and the 3D WebGL Stratigraphic Stack (z_terrain, z_vadose, z_water, z_bedrock). Click any well on the map or click 📍 Pin Well Coordinate to center and inspect specific location predictions.

4

Panel 3: Piezometric Forecast & Depletion Warnings

Expand Panel 3 to analyze multi-month piezometric head forecasts (6M to 36M) with Split Conformal uncertainty bounds (q̂). Review optimal pumping quota allocations per well and monitor real-time Depletion Warnings (h_safe, drawdown velocity m/mo, time-to-depletion, and recommended pumping cuts).
